About Us

DDS Football Management is a small, family-run agency operating primarily in Sweden and Angola. We guide and support players throughout their entire careers, whether they are based in Europe or working their way toward it.

Beyond transfers and contract negotiations, we connect players with trusted experts in nutrition, training, mental resilience, and physical well-being, always tailored to the individual. Trust, honesty, and personal attention are at the core of everything we do.

We focus on a select group of long-term clients to ensure every player gets the attention they deserve, though we are open to shorter-term arrangements when that suits best. We aim to be more than agents, dedicated, long-term partners in your football journey.

What we stand for

Integrity & Professionalism

We act with honesty and transparency at every step. Players and partners can count on us to always put their best interests first.

Long-Term Thinking

We invest in sustainable careers, not quick wins. Our focus is on building relationships and strategies that grow over time.

Individualised Support

Every player is different. We tailor our guidance, planning, and support to each person's specific needs — on and off the pitch.

Well-Being First

Physical and mental health matter as much as performance. We partner with specialists in sports psychology, physiotherapy, and nutrition for a genuinely holistic approach.

Global Network

Strong ties with clubs, coaches, and scouting networks across the world mean we can open doors that others cannot.

Passion for the Game

Football is a dream for many. We are driven by a genuine love for the sport and work every day to help players reach their full potential.
